Available options

Profile

Your verified buyer profile. Gmail is your identity for document sharing and KYC.

AI deed document scanner

Upload a Deed of Assignment, Certificate of Occupancy or survey plan. The scanner reads the document and flags forged signatures, invalid dates and registration numbers that don't match the registry.

A scan result is an indicator, not a legal opinion. Every flagged document is reviewed by our legal desk, and a clear result still requires registry confirmation before funds move.

GIS coordinates mapper

Drop a pin on the land, or paste its coordinates. We check them against the government master plan and tell you whether the parcel is free, committed to infrastructure, or zoned agricultural.

Community risk profiling

Local families and community groups are rated on the risk of double-selling, youth disruption at commencement, and litigation history in that neighbourhood.

Risk ratings describe recorded patterns in an area, not a judgement about any individual or family. They are a prompt to do deeper diligence, never a substitute for it.

Hidden fee calculator

The true cost of a plot, broken down before you commit: agency fees, legal fees, surveying, title perfection and the community development levy you should expect to budget for.

Percentages are our current standard estimates. Your final figures are confirmed in writing before any payment is made.

Milestone-based escrow

Your funds sit in escrow and are released in tranches: 20% on title verification, 40% on physical allocation, 40% on deed execution. Nothing moves until the stage is independently verified.

Escrow is held with regulated partner banks. Apexx Crestway is a technology and trust intermediary, not a bank or a licensed escrow agent in its own right.

Live construction dashboard

Independent, time-stamped and geo-located evidence of progress, streamed to you directly rather than through a relative or a realtor.

Drone and site photo evidence

Our inspectors upload time-stamped, geo-tagged drone footage and photographs against each milestone independently — customers view the results here but don't submit evidence themselves, since self-submitted "verification" wouldn't actually verify anything.

Evidence for your active milestones will appear above once an inspector visit is scheduled and completed.

Supply chain material index

Live raw material pricing across the sites we run. We track the index algorithmically and hedge bulk purchases, instead of passing an inflation premium on to you.

Digital wallet and cross-border payments

Settle from USD, GBP or EUR straight into a Naira escrow account, with the anti-money-laundering checks handled as part of the flow.

About us

Apexx Crestway Ltd

Who we are

Apexx Crestway Ltd exists to make owning premium assets in Nigeria — land, real estate and construction — safe, transparent and simple. We are the trust and execution layer standing between capital and Nigeria's most valuable, and most vulnerable, asset classes.

The problem we're solving

Deploying capital into Nigerian land and construction has always meant absorbing enormous, avoidable risk: the same plot sold to several buyers, title registration that drags on for years, fees that surface only after money has changed hands, local extortion that stalls projects before they start, and developers who promise serviced plots and deliver none of it.

These aren't edge cases. They are the default experience for buyers, investors and diaspora Nigerians trying to build wealth back home, and the result is a chaos premium baked into nearly every transaction — money lost not to bad investments but to bad information.

Our solution

Guarded land and real estate deals: we pre-vet land using GIS mapping, drone surveillance and deep-tier legal audits, then hand buyers clean, litigation-free titles instead of a gamble.

Milestone-locked construction escrow: client funds are secured in escrow and released only at verified project milestones, with live tracking so clients see progress and spending as it happens.

Our mission

To eliminate the chaos premium from Nigerian infrastructure, turning unverified land and volatile construction into transparent, institutional-grade assets that anyone — from a first-time diaspora buyer to a tier-one bank — can trust.

Why now

Nigeria's real estate sector has overtaken oil and gas as the third-largest contributor to GDP. State land registries are digitising. Document verification has become affordable. And a growing diaspora, remitting billions annually, is looking for a way to invest at home without relying on middlemen they can't fully trust.
